# Academic Writing

Use this skill to outline, draft, translate, or revise academic manuscripts.

- Confirm the section's argumentative purpose, target language, venue, audience, evidence, and length.
- Build the claim and evidence structure before polishing prose.
- Keep sourced facts, author interpretations, and proposed claims distinguishable.
- Use `[SOURCE NEEDED]`, `[DATA NEEDED]`, or `[AUTHOR DECISION]` where support is missing.
- Maintain a bilingual terminology table when working across Chinese and English.
- Adapt rhetoric and organization to the target language rather than translating literally.
- Do not change numerical values, methods, limitations, or citation meaning merely to improve fluency.
